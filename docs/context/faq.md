# Frequently asked questions

### Is OLIP an adopted standard?
No. OLIP 0.1 is a draft for review. No standards body, bar, regulator or company has adopted or endorsed it.

### Who is behind it?
OLIP was initiated and is initially sponsored by Digital Crossings Inc. The specification is proposed and open to independent contributors. See [Governance](/governance/).

### Do I need to pay anyone or use the sponsor's products to implement it?
No. You can implement OLIP without paying Digital Crossings Inc., using its software, hosting with it, or holding an account.

### Is there an "OLIP Certified" badge?
No. There is a public, free conformance suite. A pass is a dated technical result, not a certification of a lawyer or a firm.

### How does OLIP relate to MCP and A2A?
OLIP adds legal-intake meaning (evidence, eligibility, CaseBrief, consent, handoff, status). MCP and A2A carry agent calls and messages. The draft includes an MCP adapter and an A2A binding. See [Interoperability](/docs/implementing/interoperability/).

### Is there a hosted sandbox or public API at olip.ai?
No. olip.ai is documentation only. The sandbox runs on your own computer from the [kit](/spec/downloads/).

### Can an AI assistant approve sending my case on my behalf?
No. Only the person can approve, on a page the assistant cannot complete alone, for one named recipient and the exact fields shown.

### Does "delivered" mean the lawyer read it or took my case?
No. `delivered` means the firm's intake system stored it and signed a receipt. Reading, replying and accepting are separate, later events, and none of them is an attorney-client relationship.

### Does this site track me?
No analytics, cookies or third-party scripts. See [Privacy](/privacy/).

### Is the name final?
"OLIP" is a working name. A trademark clearance search has not been done yet.
