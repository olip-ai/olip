# For law firms and legal-aid organizations

OLIP lets an AI assistant describe your practice accurately and send you a request only when a person chose you and approved exactly what is sent. You stay in control of what you publish and what you accept.

## What you would publish

| Item | Your control |
|---|---|
| License evidence | Shown with its source and date. You cannot be shown as "verified" on a copied record. |
| Intake policy | Matters, places, languages, client side, exclusions, required fields, retention, and whether you are accepting new matters, all in your own words and dated. |
| Delivery | Your own intake system (direct) or a vendor you choose. |

## What you would receive

- Only the fields the person approved, with their exact values.
- A request addressed to you alone, never a bulk send.
- A clear purpose (`request_initial_contact` or `request_consultation`).

## What you would send back

- A signed receipt when your system stores the request.
- Optional events recorded by a person at your firm: acknowledged, responded, declined, consultation requested or confirmed, completed. Automatic replies never count as acknowledged.

## What OLIP does not do for you

It does not decide whether a matter is a good fit, check conflicts, form an engagement, or tell you whether a workflow complies with your jurisdiction's advertising, solicitation or referral rules. Those remain your professional responsibility.

## Cost

Implementing OLIP is free. You do not need any sponsor product, account or payment, and conformance testing is free and open. OLIP has no paid placement.

## Today

OLIP 0.1 is a draft. No firm is running an OLIP endpoint in production as of this build. If your firm or intake vendor wants to try the fictional quickstart or comment on the draft, see [Community](/community/).
