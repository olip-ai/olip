# Governance

OLIP was initiated and is initially sponsored by **Digital Crossings Inc.**, which edits the draft until an independent editorial group exists. There is no foundation, standards body, council, outside governing member or partner endorsement today. An independent governing body is **proposed**, not established; nobody has been asked to serve or has accepted a seat.

- Full governance text, decision log and the proposed path to independence: https://olip.ai/governance/ and `protocol/governance/`.
- Every decision is recorded with its date and reason in `protocol/governance/DECISION-LOG.md`.
- Normative change process: proposal issue, public comment, schema, example and test changes, compatibility impact, recorded decision. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
- Anyone may implement OLIP and run the conformance suite free, without an account with the sponsor or any company. Discovery order is disclosed and never paid. There is no certification.
