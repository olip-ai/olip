# OLIP Standards Council: proposed charter (DRAFT)

Status: proposal only. **No council exists. Nobody has been asked or has accepted a seat.** Recruiting needs the sponsor's written approval for outreach.

## Purpose
Steward the OLIP specification, schemas, conformance suite and name so that any AI agent, law firm, vendor or legal aid group can implement OLIP freely and on equal terms.

## Separation from the sponsor's business
- The sponsor (Digital Crossings Inc.) edits the draft until the council is seated, then holds **one** seat.
- Commercial decisions of the sponsor (pricing, products, advertising) are never council business, and council decisions never favor any vendor.
- Conformance does not require any sponsor account, data, brand, service or payment.

## Proposed seats (7, starting structure, subject to stakeholder agreement)
1. Practicing lawyer, solo or small firm. 2. Practicing lawyer, legal aid or public interest. 3. Independent AI agent developer (not the sponsor). 4. Legal software / practice-management vendor (not the sponsor). 5. Consumer or legal-aid advocate. 6. Security or standards expert (for example someone active in SALI, IETF or W3C work). 7. Sponsor (Digital Crossings Inc.), non-chair.

Terms of 2 years, staggered. Chair elected by members; the sponsor may not chair for the first two terms.

## Decisions
- Lazy consensus on the public issue tracker for editorial changes (7 days).
- Normative changes: public proposal, at least 14 days of comment, then a vote; passes with 5 of 7 and at least 3 non-sponsor, non-vendor seats in favor.
- Conformance-rule changes need two independent implementers to confirm testability.
- Every decision recorded in `DECISION-LOG.md` with rationale.

## Conflicts of interest
Members disclose employers, clients, investments and paid relationships with any implementer, in public, before voting. Conflicted members abstain on matters that specifically affect that party.

## Appeals and disputes
Any implementer may appeal a conformance or mark decision to the council within 30 days; two members not involved in the original decision review it. Unresolved disputes go to a neutral mediator agreed in advance.

## Path to independence
When at least 5 independent organizations run conforming implementations in production (definition in `docs/SCORECARDS.md`), the council evaluates moving the spec and mark to a neutral foundation (for example an existing open standards foundation) and the sponsor transfers the mark on agreed terms.
