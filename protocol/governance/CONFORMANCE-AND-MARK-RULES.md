# Conformance and mark rules (DRAFT)

## What a conformance result means
"Passes OLIP 0.1 olip-write conformance suite vX on <date>" means the endpoint passed those automated technical tests on that date with fictional data. It does **not** mean: the lawyer is verified, licensed in a given state, approved by any bar or regulator, compliant with referral or advertising rules, confidential, privileged, or recommended.

## Rules
1. Objective: only the published suite decides. Anyone may run it; results are reproducible JSON.
2. Nondiscriminatory: same tests and same terms for every implementer, including competitors and nonpaying firms. No fee to self-certify.
3. Truthful display: show profile, version, suite version and date, e.g. "Supports OLIP 0.1 (read, write). Self-tested 2026-11-06." Never "OLIP certified lawyer", "approved", "verified by OLIP" or a badge next to a lawyer's name that implies vetting.
4. Separate claims: technical conformance, identity evidence level, and jurisdiction permissions are three different statements and must be shown separately.
5. Expiry: a self-test result is current for 90 days or until a new suite version, whichever comes first.
6. Withdrawal: misleading use may be asked to stop; the implementer may appeal under the charter.
7. No paid ranking: no implementation may give conforming endpoints, or the sponsor's customers, preferential discovery order.
