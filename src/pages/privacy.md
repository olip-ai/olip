# Privacy statement for olip.ai

Last updated: 2026-11-07.

This statement covers the **olip.ai documentation website** only. It does not cover any OLIP implementation, which each operator describes in its own privacy policy, or any product of the sponsor.

## What this site does not do

- No analytics, tracking pixels, fingerprinting or advertising.
- No cookies.
- No third-party scripts, fonts, chat widgets or embedded content. All fonts and scripts are served from olip.ai itself.
- No accounts, logins, forms or uploads.

## What stays in your browser

- **Theme choice.** If you switch between light and dark mode, your choice is saved in your browser's local storage on your device. The site software also saves two small settings for the status banner there (`docusaurus.announcement.id` and `docusaurus.announcement.dismiss`). None of this is ever sent to us. The [Try OLIP](/try/) simulation and the [validator](/validator/) store nothing and make no network requests with what you type.
- **Search.** Search runs in your browser against an index downloaded from this site. Your search terms are not sent to a server.
- **Schema validator.** JSON you paste into the [validator](/validator/) is checked in your browser and is never transmitted. Do not paste real client or case information anyway.

## What the web host records

Like any website, the server that hosts olip.ai receives standard request information (IP address, date and time, page requested, browser type and referring page) and may keep it in access logs for security and operations. We do not combine these logs with other data or use them for marketing.

## Email

If you email us (see [Community](/community/) or [Security](/security/)), we receive your address and message and use them only to respond and to improve OLIP. Do not send real client or case information.
