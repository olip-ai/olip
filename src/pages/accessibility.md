# Accessibility

Our target is **WCAG 2.2 level AA** for this documentation site.

## What has been checked (2026-11-07)

- Color contrast of text and controls in light and dark modes against AA thresholds (body text at least 4.5:1).
- Keyboard navigation of the header, menu, search, sidebar and content, with a visible focus indicator.
- A skip-to-content link, one main heading per page, ordered headings, and text alternatives for diagrams.
- Layout at common phone widths without horizontal scrolling of the page; wide tables scroll inside their own box.
- Reduced-motion preferences are respected.

These were automated and scripted checks by the sponsor. **No audit by an independent accessibility specialist or with assistive-technology users has been done yet.**

## Known limitations

- Large tables in the specification are dense on small screens.
- Some generated tables (status machine, downloads) repeat short codes that screen readers announce letter by letter.

## Report a barrier

Email [notices@esqcopilot.com](mailto:notices@esqcopilot.com?subject=olip.ai%20accessibility) with "olip.ai accessibility" in the subject, and tell us the page and what got in your way.
